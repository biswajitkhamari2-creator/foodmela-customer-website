Welcome to FoodMela (operated by Biswajit Khamari). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules), and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data strictly necessary to facilitate online ordering and hyperlocal delivery within Birmaharajpur, Odisha:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, mobile phone number, and delivery address provided during registration or checkout.
  • Authentication Data: Mobile phone number verified through one-time password (OTP). We do NOT store passwords or sensitive verification credentials.
  • Order History: Details of items ordered, delivery status, and payment receipts to provide customer assistance, re-ordering, and billing transparency.
  • Device & Session Data: Technical logs such as IP address and browser type used strictly for security monitoring and session persistence.

2. Financial Information & Payment Security

  • No Card or PIN Storage: All digital transactions (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking) are processed directly by RBI-authorized third-party payment gateways (including PayU India). FoodMela and Biswajit Khamari never collect, view, or store your debit/credit card number, CVV, expiry date, or UPI PIN.
  • Encryption: Online transactions are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol directly through certified payment gateways.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): For cash orders, cash is paid directly to the delivery partner upon arrival. No electronic financial data is collected or retained.

3. Purpose and Use of Collected Information

  • To confirm, process, and deliver your orders accurately to your specified address in Birmaharajpur.
  • To send real-time SMS / WhatsApp order confirmations, delivery updates, and tracking notices.
  • To provide responsive customer support and resolve order or payment queries swiftly.
  • To verify user authenticity and prevent unauthorized access or fraud.

4. Zero Data Monetization & Information Sharing

We strictly do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketers or data aggregators. Data is shared only with:

  • Delivery Executives: Customer name, delivery address, and ordered items are shared solely for the purpose of completing the physical delivery.
  • Kitchen / Restaurant Partners: Ordered dishes and packaging instructions (personal customer phone numbers are masked).
  • Payment Gateways (PayU India): Order amount and transaction reference for secure payment authorization and settlement.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Only when strictly mandated by Indian law, court summons, or governmental directives.

5. Data Retention & User Account Deletion

We retain order history and contact information only as long as necessary to provide services and comply with Indian statutory accounting and taxation requirements. You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by emailing our Grievance Officer at support@foodmela.online or calling +91 8144503650. Requests are completed within 7 business days.

6. Security Measures

We deploy standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your data. All web communication is strictly transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.
