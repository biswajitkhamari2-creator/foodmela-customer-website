  1. ORDER CANCELLATION WINDOW: Customers can cancel an order with a 100% full refund only while the order is in the initial "Placed" status before the restaurant partner accepts or begins cooking. Once food preparation has begun or a delivery partner has been dispatched, cancellation is non-refundable to cover merchant and rider costs.
  2. VERIFIED TRANSIT DAMAGE OR PACKAGING ISSUES: If an order is physically damaged during transit (torn package, seal punctured, spillage, crushed container), customer must notify our helpline within 60 minutes of delivery with photographic evidence. A full replacement or pro-rata refund will be processed promptly.
  3. WRONG OR MISSING ITEMS: If an incorrect item is delivered or an ordered item is missing from a multi-item package, our support team will immediately review the delivery and issue an instant pro-rata refund or immediate redelivery of the missing item.
  4. FOOD QUALITY OR SPOILAGE ISSUES: If food arrives spoiled or stale, photo evidence must be shared with customer support within 60 minutes of delivery. Upon verification with the restaurant partner, a full refund will be granted.
  5. REFUND PROCESSING & SETTLEMENT TIMELINE:
    • Online Payments (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking): Approved refunds are processed directly back to the original source account via PayU within 24 to 48 hours (and credited to your bank account within 3 to 7 business days per standard banking cycles).
    • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Approved refunds are credited as direct UPI transfer or instant store credit according to customer preference.
  6. CASH ON DELIVERY (COD) CAPPED AT ₹100: Cash on Delivery is available for orders with total value up to ₹100. Any order exceeding ₹100 must be prepaid online via UPI or Card to ensure kitchen commitment.
  7. HELP & ESCALATION DESK: For urgent order cancellation, refund status tracking, or damage claims, call or WhatsApp our Birmaharajpur customer team at +91 8144503650 (9:00 AM – 10:00 PM) or email support@foodmela.online.
