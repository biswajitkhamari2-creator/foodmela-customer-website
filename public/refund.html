💸 Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation Policy

Cash on Delivery (COD) is permitted only for orders with total value up to ₹100. Orders exceeding ₹100 must be prepaid online via UPI or digital payment before dispatch.

Orders can be cancelled with 100% refund only while in the "Placed" stage before the kitchen accepts or begins cooking. Once food preparation has begun or the order is out for delivery, cancellations are strictly non-refundable.

Eligible Refund Scenarios (Verified Defects Only)

As per standard delivery aggregator policies, refunds or free replacements are issued only for verifiable events:

  • Packaging Damaged in Transit: Package physically torn, punctured, broken, or crushed during transit.
  • Severe Spillage or Leakage: Containers heavily leaked or spilled inside the delivery bag.
  • Wrong Item Delivered: An entirely incorrect dish or order was delivered.
  • Missing Item: An ordered item was missing from a multi-item package.
  • Food Spoiled Upon Arrival: Food arrived sour, stale, or contaminated upon delivery.

Mandatory 60-Minute Reporting: Any transit damage, spillage, or missing item must be reported within 60 minutes of receiving the order with clear photos/unboxing videos. Requests made after 60 minutes cannot be entertained.

Settlement Timeline

Approved refunds for online payments are credited directly to your original payment method within 24–48 hours (credited to bank account within 3–7 working days), or processed in cash/store credit for COD orders.
